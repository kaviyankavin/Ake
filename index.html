Origins & Heritage

The Story of Our Rice

Rooted in the fertile soils of Koppampatty, Tamil Nadu, our Seeraga Samba rice is a symbol of South Indian heritage. Cultivated in farm sanctuaries irrigated by seasonal rivers, every harvest preserves ancient methods while delivering pure, uncompromised nutrition to your dining table.

Time-Honored Sowing

Traditional Farming

Our farmers plant young seedlings by hand in traditional South Indian paddy fields, following astronomical planting calendars passed down through farming families. Hand-guided tools are used to cultivate clay soil beds gently, protecting the organic richness of the wetlands.

This traditional attention allows the small Seeraga Samba grain sprouts to mature at their own natural pace, absorbing organic minerals from the clay soil.

Organic Nurturing

Rice Cultivation

Our farming cooperatives follow natural cultivation practices. The crops are watered using gravity-fed stream channels that flow through green farm valleys. Companion species grow inside the water-filled wetlands to provide organic weed control and pest management.

By returning to classical companion-planting and natural composts, we preserve the soil structure and protect local ecosystems while yielding grains that are naturally rich in flavor and essential minerals.

Uncompromising Standards

Manual Packing & Quality Assurance

Every packet of Koppampatty Seeraga Samba Rice is carefully packed by experienced workers using traditional manual methods. Each batch is visually inspected to ensure clean, premium-quality grains before being packed in a hygienic environment. Every 1 KG and 25 KG packet is weighed accurately and sealed with care to preserve the rice's natural aroma, freshness, and quality until it reaches our customers.

Royal South Indian Dish

Koppampatty Seeraga Samba Biryani

Seeraga Samba is world-famous for biryani. Its small, fine grain size possesses an incredible capacity to absorb ghee, spices, and meat curry juices, resulting in a rich, intense flavor in every single bite.

Cooking soft, light, and non-sticky, it is highly sought after for South Indian mutton biryani. Its rich aroma fills the dining hall, turning any meal into a royal South Indian celebration.

Our Rice Journey Timeline

Follow the traditional crop cycle of Koppampatty Seeraga Samba Rice, processed with modern care.

Step 1

Nursery & Sowing

Pure seeds are carefully handpicked and sown in dedicated nutrient-rich nurseries. The sprouts are nurtured for 25 days before transplanting into water-abundant fields.

Step 2

Flooding & Weeding

Fields are naturally flooded to protect the plant roots. Local ducks are introduced to clear weeds and pests, acting as natural helpers.

Step 3

Golden Harvesting

When the stalks change from emerald green to golden, local farming experts hand-reap and dry the stalks in the natural sun.

Step 4

Traditional Aging & Milling

The rice is aged traditionally in clean, airy granaries for 12 months. Then, our experienced millers gently hull the grains and perform manual quality checks to ensure purity.
